## Claim under test

Mandatory issue diagnosis prior-art can be treated as satisfied by a matching repair subject plus any non-empty `diagnosis_prior_art_result` string, without proving that the value came from an exact consumed/applied Intake-origin Research result.

## Expected canonical semantics

Issue diagnosis prior-art is a mandatory pre-repair factual/provenance obligation. For a concrete repair subject, Intake must materialize proportional Intake-origin Research, consume and apply the exact Research result, and persist the exact subject/result binding before alignment or implementation can proceed.

A free-form mutable string that has no qualifying Research provenance cannot substitute for that completed obligation.

## Observed historical-subject behavior

At `4fb4bfb7d7b1481d6f347c182fc96a5a1135e045`:

1. `tools/state_contract.py::validate_intake()` requires `diagnosis_prior_art_result` only to be a string and, when a prior-art binding is present, non-empty.
2. The same validator requires `diagnosis_prior_art_subject == repair_subject`, but does not bind the result value to a Research record or exact Research result identity.
3. `tools/router.py` defines `stable_diagnosis_prior_art` as that subject equality plus `bool(diagnosis_prior_art_result.strip())`.
4. The exact consumed/applied Research checks are evaluated only when that predicate is false.
5. Therefore an otherwise legal authorized issue can use a value such as `forged:never-produced` or `forged-no-research`, with no qualifying Intake-origin Research record, and skip the mandatory Research/provenance path.

This is the exact behavior independently reported by S021-F02 and S032-F04.

## Technical verdict

CONFIRMED_MATERIAL

## Why

The prior-art result field is acting as authorization-relevant proof of a mandatory completed factual obligation, but its provenance is not authenticated. A fabricated non-empty value can unlock downstream repair routing even though no exact Research result was produced, applied, or consumed.

That changes workflow authority rather than only weakening diagnostics: a mandatory precondition to implementation can be bypassed by mutable text.

## Smallest relevant implementation boundary

The smallest defect boundary is the Intake diagnosis-prior-art binding shared by `tools/state_contract.py::validate_intake()` and the `stable_diagnosis_prior_art` gate in `tools/router.py`.

The binding needs provenance to the exact qualifying consumed/applied Intake-origin Research result; a non-empty string alone is insufficient.

## Useful future regression-test shape

1. Concrete authorized issue + matching prior-art subject + arbitrary non-empty result + no Research record -> fail closed or route the mandatory Research/reconciliation obligation.
2. Same state with a consumed Research record whose origin subject differs -> fail closed.
3. Same state with matching Research subject but different `return_result` from the Intake binding -> fail closed.
4. Exact consumed/applied Intake-origin Research for the current repair subject, followed by persistence of its exact result binding -> legal continuation.
5. Reusing the Research slot after the exact binding is persisted must not erase the already-proven provenance.
