## Claim under test

A materially changed Master Plan can self-classify as `editorial_exempt` using only metadata plus a non-empty prose basis, preserve prior Plan Review/premium-B/premium-C coverage, and proceed without the new independent review cycle required for material planning changes.

## Expected canonical semantics

PWV2-REQ-039 requires every new or materially revised Master Plan to receive independent Plan Review. Only mechanical/editorial changes that do **not** alter strategy, milestone structure, requirement coverage, or accepted gates may omit that review.

`workflow/PLANNING.md` carries the same boundary: material correction creates a new planning cycle/revision and repeats the required review/premium sequence; only a genuinely editorial/mechanical correction may reuse prior GREEN review and satisfied C.

## Observed historical-subject behavior

At `4fb4bfb7d7b1481d6f347c182fc96a5a1135e045`:

1. `validate_planning()` accepts `review_mode = "editorial_exempt"` when the plan is approved, the new exact subject differs from the prior reviewed base, the exemption basis is merely non-empty, and prior B/C subjects remain bound to that base.
2. The validator receives no old/new plan bytes or structured strategy/milestone/coverage/gate comparison from which it could establish that the change is actually editorial.
3. `validate_plan_review()` then requires the retained prior GREEN review to match the exemption base subject, not the newly changed plan subject.
4. The router accepts that configuration and can continue to Execution Prep without a new Stage-6 Plan Review.
5. The repository's positive `test_editorial_plan_exemption_preserves_prior_green_subject` changes the plan blob, supplies a prose “wording only” basis, and expects validation success without demonstrating the semantic boundedness of the changed content.
6. The preserved S023-F03 reproducer constructs exactly that shape with a new plan blob and retained prior B/C/review subject; the production validators accept it without any plan-content input.

Therefore a materially different plan is indistinguishable at this gate from a wording-only change if the planning record self-attests the exemption.

## Technical verdict

CONFIRMED_MATERIAL

## Why

The defect is not that semantic classification must necessarily be automated. The defect is that a MUST-level review exception whose legality depends on semantic facts is authorized solely by the same mutable planning record that benefits from the exception, with no independently bound or content-derived proof of those facts.

This creates a direct bypass of the mandatory independent Plan Review for material plan revisions. A new exact plan subject can carry changed strategy while reusing the previous GREEN/B/C chain if its metadata says the change is editorial.

## Smallest likely defect boundary

The smallest defect boundary is the authorization/proof seam for `editorial_exempt`.

The workflow needs a durable basis that is not merely self-attested by the exemption-bearing planning record and that binds the classification to the exact prior and changed plan subjects. The exact mechanism could be independent semantic classification, a sufficiently structured machine-verifiable invariant surface, or another accepted authority mechanism; this audit does not prescribe the repair.

## Useful regression-test shape

Use two exact prior/current plan subjects and prior valid GREEN/B/C coverage:

1. Pure mechanical/editorial change with durable bounded classification tied to both exact subjects -> exemption is accepted.
2. Change in strategy, milestone structure, requirement coverage, or accepted gates -> exemption is rejected and a new material Planning/Plan Review cycle is required.
3. New blob + only a self-authored non-empty “wording only” basis -> fail closed; it must not authorize Execution Prep.
4. Prior GREEN subject mismatch -> fail closed.
5. Stale or unbound exemption proof -> fail closed.
6. Positive editorial case must demonstrate why the semantic invariant is preserved rather than merely set a prose field.
