# Research

## Purpose

Research produces source-grounded evidence for requirements, decisions or planning.

Research is evidence, not accepted project intent by itself.

## Inputs

Read:
- the exact research question;
- only accepted requirements/decisions that constrain it;
- relevant source/runtime/project material;
- external sources when current or outside evidence is required.

## Method

- distinguish observed facts from inference and recommendation;
- verify current external facts when freshness matters;
- preserve source provenance;
- record uncertainty/conflicting evidence;
- do not silently convert research findings into accepted product/architecture decisions.

## Outputs

Persist durable findings under `research/` when they materially affect later work.

A useful research artifact states:
- question;
- sources/evidence;
- findings;
- uncertainty/limitations;
- implications/options;
- decisions still required.

## Transition

Research may inform brainstorming, requirements, decisions or planning.

If the evidence requires changing already accepted authority during active implementation, treat that as a strategic decision rather than quietly rewriting downstream contracts.
