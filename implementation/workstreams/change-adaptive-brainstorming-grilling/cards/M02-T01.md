# M02-T01 — Make adaptive grilling intrinsic to every Brainstorming route

- Milestone: `M02`

> This file is a stable Task Card contract. Mutable execution/review/result state lives only in the selected manifest-bound workstream Task Board.

## Authority slice

- Master Plan / milestone contract: `planning/BRAINSTORMING_GRILLING_MASTER_PLAN.md#M02--make-adaptive-grilling-intrinsic-to-every-brainstorming-route` — revision `BGR-P3`, approved.
- Requirements: `requirements/BRAINSTORMING_GRILLING.md` revision `R2`, `BGR-REQ-001..018`.
- Accepted decisions: `decisions/ADR_BRAINSTORMING_ADAPTIVE_GRILLING.md` / `ADR-BGR-002`.

- Accepted dependency results: integrated historical M01 baseline, including `implementation/workstreams/feature-brainstorming-grilling/evidence/M01_FINAL_CLOSURE_2026-09-20.md`.

### Must preserve

- Adaptive grilling is intrinsic to every active Brainstorming route and remains an interaction method inside Brainstorming, never a new phase, Intake/workstream kind, scheduler, questionnaire engine, or authority layer.
- Depth is governed by expected decision value, not a fixed minimum/maximum question or round count. A simple scope may terminate quickly only after the required completion audit/final challenge; implementation readiness alone is insufficient.
- Material unresolved user/product/strategic decisions use a dependency-aware tree/frontier; prerequisites are settled before dependent questions are exposed; answers recompute the frontier.
- Relevant decision lenses are searched adaptively and internally rather than exposed as a rigid user-facing checklist.
- User-facing questions are thematic and digestible, numbered, and each material decision question includes an explicit assistant recommendation.
- Each material settled choice receives one bounded counterfactual challenge. It is reopened only by materially new evidence, contradiction, or changed context.
- Agent-findable facts remain agent-owned and use each route's normal Research contract, reconciling exactly once into the same exploratory subject before Brainstorming resumes.
- Normal completion requires a bounded relevant-surface audit plus one final challenge/discovery pass.
- Clear natural-language user stop halts new questions immediately; unresolved material blockers keep the exploratory state tentative and prevent `ready_for_definition`.
- Durable exploratory state remains concise recovery state; do not create a second persisted decision-tree/transcript authority.
- Existing explicit user-owned Brainstorming → Project Definition promotion remains unchanged.
- Remove `#grill` completely from active supported interaction surfaces with no compatibility alias. Preserve historical M01/OpenSpec/evidence as provenance rather than rewriting it.
- Equivalent interaction semantics must cover ChatGPT-only, Codex-only, and legacy/mixed Brainstorming while retaining policy-local Research/recovery/lifecycle mechanics.
- Preserve `#issue` / `#feature` Intake semantics.
- `wait-what` remains outside scope.
- No new credentials, privileged runtime, persistent data migration, mutable global state, or external live-write behavior.

### Must not / rationale that must travel

- Do not solve shallow Brainstorming by adding an arbitrary numeric depth quota or a generic runtime questionnaire engine; R2 explicitly chooses adaptive expected decision value.
- Do not keep a hidden/manual `#grill` compatibility path; that would recreate two conceptual interaction modes.
- Do not update only migrated fixed-policy routes; legacy/mixed is an accepted active route family.
- Do not rewrite the already-integrated M01 evidence/OpenSpec merely to make history look current.
- Do not weaken Research ownership or the Definition-promotion gate while simplifying the interaction contract.

## Dependencies

- Approved BGR-P3 and GREEN independent plan review.
- Integrated historical M01 baseline.

## Outcome

All active Project Workflow Brainstorming routes expose one coherent adaptive-default grilling method that resists shallow early completion, removes active `#grill`, preserves existing authority/lifecycle boundaries, and has deterministic verification that distinguishes a genuinely simple short exploration from dependency-rich multi-round discovery.

## Acceptance

- ChatGPT-only, Codex-only, and legacy/mixed Brainstorming all express adaptive-default semantics; no entry route decides whether grilling happens.
- Dependency-aware frontier/recomputation, adaptive internal lenses, thematic batching, numbering and recommendation-per-material-question are explicit.
- Every material settled choice gets one bounded counterfactual challenge and is stable absent materially new evidence/contradiction/context.
- Researchable facts use normal agent-owned Research and resume the same exploratory subject.
- Normal completion requires the completion audit and one final discovery/challenge pass; implementation readiness alone cannot end Brainstorming.
- No numeric depth quota or generic questionnaire runtime is introduced.
- Deterministic scenario evidence proves a simple scope can end after a short audited round and a dependency-rich scope proceeds through successive recomputed rounds.
- Clear user stop ends new questions immediately while material unresolved blockers keep the scope tentative.
- Recovery remains possible from concise durable exploratory state without persisting the full transient tree/transcript.
- Active routers, Intake, Brainstorming contracts, current docs/examples, current OpenSpec and current BGR tests no longer support or advertise `#grill`; historical provenance remains readable.
- Explicit Brainstorming → Project Definition promotion and policy-local lifecycle mechanics remain intact.
- All BGR-REQ-001..018 are represented in implementation/verification and the repository regression suite remains GREEN.
