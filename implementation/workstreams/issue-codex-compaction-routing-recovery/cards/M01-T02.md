- The exact schema/kernel accepted by M01-T01 and its independent review.
- New manifests establish a usable binding before Intake completion and before post-materialization policy-dependent runtime realization.
- A pre-schema branch-first manifest with no `orchestration` block uses one bounded Recovery upgrade; a present invalid/unknown binding remains fail-closed.
- Every actual policy-dependent realization/re-realization goes through one generic role-agnostic pre-dispatch boundary.
- Runtime owns opaque policy interpretation and concrete role→harness/model/worker lifecycle.
- Existing independent review, ownership, execution-policy and real-stop semantics remain canonically owned.
- `codex_only` remains continuous; no Context Health/FRESH hygiene stop is introduced.

### Must not / rationale that must travel

- Do not duplicate a role→harness/model map in Project Workflow.
- Do not silently fall back to native/internal harness while the bound runtime owner is installed/enabled and re-bind cannot be proven.
- Do not persist concrete runtime identity or a durable current-context latch.
- Do not broaden recovery into a whole-tree or external-runtime-repository reread.
- Do not change `chatgpt_only` behavior.

## Acceptance

- New selected workstreams cannot complete Intake or realize a policy-dependent worker after manifest materialization without a usable opaque binding.
- Pre-schema branch-first manifests gain the binding through a bounded fail-closed Recovery transition; already-present invalid bindings are not silently repaired into another policy.
- Reconstruction/uncertainty treats the current-context latch as absent/uncertain and re-binds before first policy-dependent realization even with unchanged fingerprint.
- Executor, Tester, plan-review Tester, Investigator, bounded-parallel launch and Recovery retry/replacement paths all inherit one generic pre-dispatch gate.
- Project Workflow contains no duplicated concrete role→harness/model map.
- Failed re-bind routes the normal concrete runtime blocker without changing execution policy.
- Bounded read/rebind behavior remains token-light and `codex_only` deterministic continuation/true-stop semantics remain intact.
