### Must preserve

- Root `PROJECT.md` remains integrated-project navigation only and must not regain active workstream-local mutable routing pointers.
- The selected workstream manifest `routing.exploratory_scope`, `routing.research_obligation`, and `routing.plan_review` are locators only; pointed artifacts retain their lifecycle/status/subject/result ownership.
- Brainstorming → Definition promotion remains explicitly user-owned and must be authorized by the exact currently pointed exploratory record/revision.
- Pre-execution Research must remain crash-safe through one exact locator plus record-owned Origin/Return/reconciliation state.
- Plan review remains an independent exact-plan-subject lifecycle; fresh-chat independence and locator-only handoff semantics are preserved.
- Branch-first, exact-branch recovery, fail-closed locator validation and no-global-registry invariants from M01 remain in force.
- ChatGPT-only lifecycle mechanics must not import Codex-only modules or semantics.

### Must not / rationale that must travel

Do not satisfy this Card by merely renaming root PROJECT fields or duplicating workstream locators back into root state. Do not perform the execution/default-board migration owned by M02-T02 except for the minimum routing boundary necessary to keep pre-execution continuation coherent.

ChatGPT-only Brainstorming, pre-execution Research, Project Definition, Planning and Plan Review recover and mutate active routing only through the exact selected workstream manifest/pointed records, never through root PROJECT mutable pointers.

## Acceptance

1. No active ChatGPT-only Brainstorming/Definition/Planning route reads or writes `PROJECT.md → Active exploratory scope`.
2. No pre-execution ChatGPT-only Research handoff/return reads or writes `PROJECT.md → Active research obligation`; selected manifest `routing.research_obligation` owns the locator.
3. The Definition promotion gate resolves the exact exploratory record through selected manifest `routing.exploratory_scope` and remains user-owned.
4. Plan-review activation/recovery uses selected manifest `routing.plan_review` plus the exact review record; completed/stale pointers are reconciled deterministically.
5. Pre-execution Research remains crash-safe and record-owned for Status/Origin/Return/reconciliation.
6. Missing/malformed/wrong-subject workstream locators fail closed to Recovery rather than falling back to root/global inference.
7. Fresh review/recovery handoffs remain branch-aware and locator-only.
8. ChatGPT-only files do not import Codex-only lifecycle modules.
9. OpenSpec and focused scenario/static checks are GREEN.
