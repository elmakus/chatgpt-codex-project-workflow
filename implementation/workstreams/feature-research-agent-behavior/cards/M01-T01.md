### Must preserve

- Research remains evidence-producing and never silently creates accepted requirements, decisions, plans or execution authority.
- Use the smallest evidence path sufficient for the exact question; broad external discovery is not required for purely local/private facts when prior art cannot materially help.
- When relevant public prior art is reasonably likely to exist and materially help, Research actively searches for it before defaulting to an original solution.
- Source classes may include official/upstream docs/source, changelogs, issues/discussions, comparable implementations and community reports as relevant.
- Stronger/primary evidence takes precedence when evidence conflicts; anecdotal/community evidence remains useful for practical failure modes and workarounds but is not authoritative by popularity.
- Conflicting evidence, assumptions, uncertainties and unresolved authority questions are surfaced explicitly.
- Existing Origin/Return-target/reconciliation lifecycle and branch-first pointer ownership remain unchanged.
- ChatGPT-only and Codex-only receive equivalent evidence-quality behavior.
- Concrete Codex Investigator model/harness/session realization remains owned by `codex_workflow`.
- External discovery is bounded and stops once sufficient source-grounded evidence answers the exact question and meaningful alternatives.
- When required external access is unavailable, report the concrete limitation rather than fabricating evidence.
- No crawler, search index/database, new workflow phase/state owner, or root-level active Research pointer may be introduced.

### Must not / rationale that must travel

Do not implement a source-ranking engine, exhaustive source checklist, mandatory Reddit/forum lookup, or Project Workflow-owned Codex Investigator runtime mapping. The accepted behavior is proportional evidence discovery, not new research infrastructure.
