### F1 — the per-class breaker still counts closure subjects that do not represent a repair round

Load-bearing surface: PWV21-REQ-113/PWV21-REQ-137 and Card acceptance requiring a default ceiling of **3 failed repair→closure-verification rounds per material defect class**.

`tools/review_contract.py::review_convergence_state` now deduplicates RED closure attempts by `_review_subject_identity`, but that identity is merely `(repository, commit, path, blob)`. The implementation never proves that the counted closure subject is a repaired content state relative to its source discovery.

Two concrete false-count paths remain:

1. A RED discovery reviews subject S0. Two real repairs produce S1 and S2 and receive RED closure verification. A third RED closure over S0 is counted as a third distinct round even though S0 is the original unrepaired discovery subject. The current router regression fixture `test_third_failed_closure_round_routes_review_convergence` explicitly blesses this case: discovery uses the default `"b" * 40` blob, while the three RED closure attempts use `"4" * 40`, `"5" * 40`, and again `"b" * 40`. The breaker therefore fires after only two actual repaired content states.
2. Because commit is part of the dedup key, the same result content/blob under a different commit can be counted as another failed repair round even though no material repair changed the reviewed artifact.

This is not a cosmetic accounting detail: it can force Main convergence/root-cause mode one repair round early, violating the accepted breaker semantics.

Required correction: derive failed repair→closure rounds from distinct **repaired content states**, not merely distinct commit-qualified review subjects. At minimum, a counted closure round must differ in reviewed content from its source discovery subject, and the same repository/path/blob content must not count twice merely because the commit changed. Add negative fixtures for source-subject reuse and same-blob/different-commit reuse, plus positive coverage showing three genuinely distinct repaired contents still trigger the breaker.
