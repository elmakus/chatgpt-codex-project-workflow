### F1 — epoch reset can be authorized by arbitrary prose rather than a material accepted redesign

Load-bearing surface: PWV21-REQ-111 and Card acceptance requiring ordinary repair to preserve the epoch and permitting reset only for a **material accepted authority/acceptance redesign** with durable reset basis, including negative epoch-reset-abuse coverage.

In `tools/state_contract.py::validate_review_history`, a changed epoch is accepted when `bool(reset_basis.strip())` is true. No accepted authority/acceptance redesign identity or other mechanically/semantically bindable accepted-redesign evidence is required. Therefore a pending E02 attempt with `epoch_reset_basis = "repair convenience"` is accepted even though ordinary repair is explicitly forbidden from resetting the epoch.

The focused test `test_review_epoch_reset_requires_material_accepted_redesign_basis` rejects an empty reset basis and rejects a non-empty reset basis on an unchanged epoch, but it does not test the actual abuse case: changing E01→E02 with a non-empty basis that is not an accepted redesign.

Required correction: make epoch reset evidence bind to a durable accepted authority/acceptance redesign rather than accepting arbitrary non-empty prose, and add a negative fixture proving ordinary-repair/convenience text cannot reset the epoch.

### F2 — T01-adapted known defect classes are later miscounted as genuinely new discovery epochs

Load-bearing surface: PWV21-REQ-112/PWV21-REQ-137, Card acceptance that recurrence/reopening of an already-known class must not consume a discovery epoch, and the required M02R-T01 transition compatibility.

`review_convergence_state` intentionally ignores pre-convergence history and updates `seen_defect_classes` only from convergence-aware RED discovery attempts. The T01 compatibility path in `validate_review_history` allows the first convergence-aware closure to introduce `material_defect_class_ids` for an open explicit T01 RED discovery, but that closure does not seed the derived known-class set.

Concrete counterexample:
1. explicit T01 RED discovery R01 freezes finding F1 without convergence fields;
2. convergence-aware closure R02 for F1 records class-a in E01 and closes it;
3. the next fresh full-scope RED R03 reopens class-a and nothing else.

At step 3, `review_convergence_state` sees class-a as unseen and increments `discovery_epochs` to 1. That is a recurrence of an already-known class, so the counter must remain 0 for the convergence-aware epoch. The current T01 compatibility fixture only proves that the closure shape validates; it does not test recurrence accounting after adaptation.

Required correction: seed known defect-class identity from the T01→T02 adaptation without retroactively inventing a discovery epoch, and add a focused recurrence fixture proving that the adapted class does not consume a later discovery epoch.

### F3 — the 3-round breaker counts repeated RED closure attempts even when no new repair occurred

Load-bearing surface: PWV21-REQ-113/PWV21-REQ-137 and Card acceptance requiring a default ceiling of **3 failed repair→closure-verification rounds per material defect class**.

`review_convergence_state` increments `failed_closure_rounds[class]` for every RED `closure_verification` attempt. Neither that helper nor `validate_review_history` requires a new repaired subject/repair transition before another failed round is counted.

The focused router fixture makes the defect explicit: `test_third_failed_closure_round_routes_review_convergence` appends R02/R03/R04 RED closure attempts using the helper's same default subject blob for all three attempts and expects `review_convergence`. That proves the implementation currently counts three repeated reviews of one unchanged repair subject as three failed repair→closure rounds.

Required correction: a failed round must be tied to a distinct repaired subject (or an equivalently durable repair-round identity) before it increments the per-class breaker. Add negative coverage showing repeated RED closure reviews of the same exact subject do not consume additional repair/closure rounds, plus positive coverage for three distinct repaired subjects.
