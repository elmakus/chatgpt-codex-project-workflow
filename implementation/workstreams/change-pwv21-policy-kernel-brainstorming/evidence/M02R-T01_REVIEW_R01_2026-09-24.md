### F1 — New PWv2.1 review attempts can bypass the explicit discovery contract

Load-bearing surface: explicit discovery-vs-closure semantics, complete-pass discovery, and mandatory fresh rediscovery before GREEN.

`tools/state_contract.py` allows any attempt that omits `review_kind` and all other PWv2.1 fields. `tools/review_contract.py::review_kind()` then unconditionally interprets that attempt as `discovery`, and `can_finalize_review_obligation()` accepts a GREEN legacy-shaped attempt. `tools/router.py` therefore routes such a newly-created GREEN attempt directly to `post_review_finalization`.

The compatibility rule is intended only for historical attempts that predate the explicit fields, but the implementation contains no durable predicate that distinguishes historical records from newly-created attempts. A new attempt can consequently omit `discovery_complete` and `material_finding_ids` and still satisfy the review obligation.

Required correction: preserve historical compatibility while making omission of the PWv2.1 review-kind fields invalid for new attempts/current PWv2.1 lifecycle state, with negative state/router coverage proving that a newly-created legacy-shaped GREEN attempt cannot finalize.

### F2 — GREEN closure of only a subset of a discovery's known findings advances to rediscovery too early

Load-bearing surface: all known material findings from one discovery must be closure-verified before the mandatory fresh full-scope rediscovery gate.

`validate_review_history()` explicitly permits a closure attempt whose `material_finding_ids` are any subset of the source RED discovery findings. The added state test validates `R01 = [F1,F2]` followed by GREEN closure `R02 = [F1]`. `tools/router.py` then treats any GREEN `closure_verification` as sufficient to route to `review_freeze` for a new fresh discovery attempt.

That permits rediscovery while `F2` is still a known unclosed material finding, contrary to the Task Card, REQ-137 and ADR-PWV21-004.

Required correction: track/derive cumulative closure of the source discovery set and permit transition to fresh rediscovery only when every frozen material finding from the applicable RED discovery is verified closed. Add negative history/router tests for partial closure and positive coverage for complete closure, whether closure is one attempt or multiple supported attempts.
