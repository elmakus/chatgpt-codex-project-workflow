## Material findings

### F1 — RED discovery can omit all load-bearing evidence

Load-bearing surface: PWV21-REQ-133 and the Card's evidence-gated blocking-finding acceptance.

`tools/review_contract.py:448-450` defines an observation-aware attempt by the truthiness of `finding_severity`, `observations` or `observation_updates`. `tools/state_contract.py:936-945` requires severity coverage for a RED discovery only inside that conditional. A terminal PWv2.1 discovery with `material_finding_ids = ["F1"]`, `verdict = "red"`, and no `finding_severity` or observations passes `validate_review`; the same is true when the three arrays are present but empty. Thus a new RED may block GREEN without naming a load-bearing surface or evidence at all. The tests exercise an already-aware attempt with a separate observation but omit this empty/absent-carrier path.

Required correction: make load-bearing evidence mandatory for each terminal PWv2.1 RED discovery without depending on nonempty advisory records, while retaining the intentional historical V2 compatibility path. Add negative fixtures for absent and empty severity arrays on a new RED discovery.

### F2 — Observation provenance can point away from its originating review

Load-bearing surface: PWV21-REQ-134 and the Card's durable traceability requirement.

`tools/review_contract.py:604-606` passes the observation's own `evidence` value as both arguments to `validate_observation_provenance`; `derive_observation_state` later stores `attempt.evidence_path` as origin but does not bind the observation evidence to that file (`tools/review_contract.py:699-724`). A valid GREEN attempt with `evidence_path = "evidence/review-R01.md"` and observation `evidence = "unrelated/R99.md#O1"` passes `validate_review` and `derive_observation_state`. The resulting durable record claims origin R01 while its only concrete observation pointer leads to R99. No source evidence readback or linkage check rejects the mismatch.

Required correction: bind each observation's evidence locator or equivalent concrete reference to the originating terminal attempt's review evidence; test mismatched paths and missing source evidence. Keep trackers excluded as provenance.

### F3 — Pre-Final reconciliation accepts an incomplete observation set

Load-bearing surface: PWV21-REQ-135 and the Card's pre-Final no-open-observation gate.

`tools/close_contract.py:293-335` validates only the caller-supplied `observations` iterable. It neither derives nor requires a completeness proof against durable review-attempt history. In the probe, `derive_observation_state([R01])` contained an open `O1`, but `verify_final_observation_reconciliation(observations=[])` returned `final_observation_reconciliation_complete`. `close_continuation(approved_scope_durably_complete=True, next_authorized_obligation=False, explicit_authorization_gate_due=False)` also returned `end_of_scope_stop` without consulting this reconciliation. The M05 plan may later integrate full Card/Milestone/Final history, but the BOOT-A gate delivered by this Card must not certify an omitted open observation.

Required correction: make the pre-Final check consume canonical derived history or a completeness-bound state snapshot, and add a negative fixture in which a known open observation is omitted from a proposed Final input. Full M05 lifecycle integration is not requested here.

### F4 — Cleanup is accepted without its exact subject, tests or reviewed evidence

Load-bearing surface: PWV21-REQ-136 and the Card's requirement that bounded cleanup has its own exact subject, tests/evidence and independent review.

`validate_cleanup_work` checks those properties in `tools/close_contract.py:231-290`, but `verify_final_observation_reconciliation` never invokes or binds that validator. At `tools/close_contract.py:337-365`, a cleanup entry with only `work_id`, `covers_observation_ids = ["O1"]`, `complete = True`, and `independent_review_green = True` covers a `cleanup_candidate` and returns `final_observation_reconciliation_complete`. It has no subject, test/evidence reference or independent review attempt; speculative-redesign and new-scope flags would also be ignored there. This allows Final to treat a bare boolean claim as completed reviewed cleanup.

Required correction: bind covering cleanup work to the validated exact cleanup subject, evidence and independent GREEN review before the Final gate accepts it; test omission and speculative/new-scope claims through the gate itself. Preserve the rule that merely conceivable further advisory improvement does not reopen completed cleanup.
