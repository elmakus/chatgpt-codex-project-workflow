# M02Q-T22 pre-execution contract correction

Launch refresh caught an over-strong wording in the just-materialized T22 Card before any product mutation occurred. Durable OBL-M02Q-01 requires H017 target recovery coverage of an accepted cleanup work's independent Review and evidence, or Final must reject that cleanup work as package-absent. The cleanup implementation subject itself may be a cross-repository Git subject proved by the Review and is not necessarily a target-side recovery artifact.

Therefore the Card is corrected before substantive Execution to require exact package membership for the cleanup independent Review plus its terminal/tests evidence, while preserving exact subject proof through existing H019 validation. No product bytes were changed under the over-strong wording, no result exists, and no review subject was frozen.
