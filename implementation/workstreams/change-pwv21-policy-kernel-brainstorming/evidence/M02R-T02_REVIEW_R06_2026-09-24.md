### F1 — Card review can self-declare a lower review scope and consume the wrong discovery ceiling

Load-bearing surface: PWV21-REQ-112, PWV21-REQ-113, the Card acceptance requiring scope-aware Card=5/Milestone=4/Final=3 discovery ceilings, and deterministic convergence mode-switching.

`tools/state_contract.py::validate_review` validates `review_scope` only as one of `card|milestone|final`. `validate_review_history` receives `expected_card_id` for the exact selected Card but never binds the convergence-aware `review_scope` to that Card-level review obligation. The Card router path calls that validator and then `tools/review_contract.py::review_convergence_state` selects the hard ceiling directly from the serialized `review_scope`.

Therefore an otherwise valid Card review history may declare `review_scope = "final"`. It still passes Card identity/history validation, but its discovery ceiling becomes 3 instead of the required Card ceiling 5. Three fresh RED discoveries with genuinely new material defect classes can consequently route the Card to `review_convergence` two discovery epochs early. The inverse class of mismatch can also delay convergence by selecting a larger scope than the actual review owner once non-Card review owners are integrated.

This makes the 5/4/3 matrix caller-controlled rather than bound to the real review obligation and breaks deterministic scope-aware accounting.

Required correction:
- bind convergence `review_scope` to the actual review obligation owner instead of trusting the attempt's self-declared scope;
- at minimum, Card-owned review history validated with `expected_card_id` must require `review_scope = "card"`;
- preserve a reusable explicit expected-scope seam for later Milestone/Final integration rather than inferring those scopes from free attempt data;
- add a negative state-contract fixture proving a Card attempt cannot claim `milestone` or `final`;
- add a router regression showing a Card cannot trigger the Final=3 or Milestone=4 breaker by changing only `review_scope`;
- preserve the positive Card/Milestone/Final ceiling matrix at the generic convergence-accounting layer.
