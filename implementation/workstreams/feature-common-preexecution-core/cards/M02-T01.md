## Included scope

Implement the first usable common Intake/alignment slice without importing V1 policy trees:
- common `workflow/INTAKE.md` and the smallest supporting state/router semantics;
- branch-first exact workstream recovery/dedup for new managed intent;
- `#feature` discovery route and neutral managed-intent route;
- `#issue` diagnosis-only entry: marker alone cannot authorize implementation;
- durable explicit repair-alignment state sufficient to distinguish pending alignment from authorized repair;
- changed repair subject invalidates stale alignment;
- qualified micro-fix may be classified only after aligned repair authorization;
- exact real-stop representation for alignment/promotion/premium/explicit-user boundaries needed by this slice;
- production-semantic fixtures/tests for symptom-only issue, question/concern response, explicit aligned authorization, changed diagnosis/stale alignment and restart recovery.
