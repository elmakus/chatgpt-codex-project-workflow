# Live review authority — M

Authority purpose: clean runtime-neutral independent-review evidence

Accepted requirement:

- exact reviewed result content MUST be `review-m: GOOD\n`;
- review must be read-only with respect to the immutable subject;
- the reviewing context must not have materially produced or repaired the reviewed subject;
- canonical Project Workflow review evidence may contain only semantic facts required to recover and trust the review;
- canonical evidence MUST NOT contain concrete product, worker-role, model, session, invocation, workspace or worktree identity merely to prove independence.

A GREEN verdict is correct only when all requirements above are satisfied.
